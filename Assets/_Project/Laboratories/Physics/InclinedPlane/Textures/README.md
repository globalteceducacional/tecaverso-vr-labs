# Madeira da caixa de carga

`CrateWood.png`: textura criada pelo `imagegen` integrado em 15/10/2026, copiada para o projeto e aplicada ao material `CrateWood.mat`. Importação limitada a 1024 px, mipmaps, filtro trilinear, anisotropia 4 e compressão. A geometria do prefab `WoodenCargoCrate` fornece as tábuas, travessas diagonais e cantoneiras; a imagem contém somente a superfície da madeira.

Prompt utilizado:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: square seamless tileable albedo texture for wooden planks of a small shipping crate in a clean educational Unity VR laboratory. Primary request: warm natural pine wood grain, subtle medium honey tan, restrained stylized realism, elongated fine grain running horizontally, sparse small knots, matte unfinished timber. Orthographic flat surface filling every pixel, even diffuse color with NO baked directional lighting, no ambient occlusion, no perspective, no borders, no seams between boards, no nails, no lettering, no logos, no objects. One continuous wood surface intended to be mapped onto individual geometric planks. Moderate low contrast so colorful force arrows remain the focus. 1024 square.
